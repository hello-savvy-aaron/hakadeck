# Map-Pack Plan — why 17 deck builders outrank a 5.0★/90-review listing

Built 2026-10-01 from the "deck builders near me" Google Maps list Aaron
pasted (22 listings, Haka #17), re-run from four service-area cities with
Google's Local Finder, a read of Haka's own Google Business Profile, Semrush
backlink + organic pulls on eight competitor domains (~1,250 API units), and
seven website audits saved in `seo/research/map-pack-2026-10/`. Companion to
`improvement-plan-2026-10.md` (which already names the map pack as the miss)
and `owner-checklist.md`. Everything in **bold** is owner-side.

## TL;DR

1. **It is not the reviews.** Haka's 5.0★/90 is the best rating-×-count in
   the whole list, and Grand View Deck & Patio sits in the Centennial 3-pack
   with **8** reviews. Google's own ranking doc says local rank =
   relevance × distance × prominence; Haka is losing on *prominence* (web
   authority, citations, brand consistency) and on *profile activity*, not
   on star rating.
2. **Haka's listing is stale.** Last GBP post Jul 16; the newest visible
   review is ~3 months old (June — the middle of build season); owner
   replies went out in one batch "2 months ago". Competitors post and
   collect reviews weekly.
3. **The rebrand is still half-done in Google's eyes.** Reviews say "Haka
   Construction", hakaconstruction.com was still indexed as "Haka
   Construction: HOME" today, Yelp has Haka under "Patio Coverings",
   Nextdoor says "Englewood", and BBB/Apple/Angi still carry old data. The
   site's authority (AS 6, 142 referring domains, nearly all spam) is a
   quarter of the Centennial neighbours' (AS 19–24, 420–650 domains).
4. **Three of the slots above Haka are spam.** "Denver Deck Builders" runs
   **four** profiles on one phone number ("Open 24 hours") from a private
   house and an apartment building; two other listings are pinned in Utah
   and Florida. Reporting them is legitimate and removes 5–6 listings that
   sit above Haka in every city.
5. **Where you search from decides the list.** Aaron's paste was taken
   ~11 miles north-east of Haka's shop (best-fit centre ≈ 39.74, −104.81),
   which is why Aurora shops filled the top three. Re-run from Centennial,
   Haka is **#15**; from Littleton, Highlands Ranch and Parker it is **not
   in the top 20**.

## 2. Why each listing ranks above Haka

Google's published factors are **relevance** (how well the profile matches
the query), **distance** (from the searcher), and **prominence** ("how
well-known a business is … information Google has about a business from
across the web, like links, articles and directories … your position in web
results is also a factor"). Everything below maps to one of the three.

   page by name (composite decks, deck replacement, deck repair, deck

   Centennial / Denver Tech Center, and the service cities; secondary
   categories "Contractor" and "Patio builder" (keep Deck builder primary);
   attributes (owner-operated, free estimates, onsite services); Q&A —
   post and answer the six FAQ questions from the cost guide.
4. **Hours.** Competitors list 7–8 PM; four say 24 hours (don't copy that).
   If Pete answers the phone until 7, set 7; Google only asks for "regular

5. **Address.** Google's rule: a service-area business "should hide your
   business address from customers". If homeowners don't visit 9707 E
   Easter Ln, clear the address field. It does not change distance ranking
   (Google keeps the location) and it removes a suspension risk; if the
   shop does receive customers, leave it.
6. **Photos.** Keep adding, but tag them: upload from the job in the town,
   caption with the town and the product.
