## The first town east on I-70

Bennett sits at about 5,480 feet on the prairie forty minutes east of the Tech Center, a farm and railroad town of roughly 3,000 people that has become a commuter town. Old Town Bennett near the tracks is 1900s–70s houses on a small grid; Muegge Farms, Bennett Crossing, and Antelope Hills are 2010s–2020s production subdivisions on flat, wind-swept ground, and the Kiowa-Bennett corridor runs south into acreage. Town limits straddle the Adams/Arapahoe county line, which matters for a lot of things but not, inside town, for permits.

## The town issues its own permits

Unlike unincorporated [Watkins](/locations/watkins) and [Strasburg](/locations/strasburg) next door, which file with the counties, Bennett is an incorporated town with its own Building Department under Community Development. Applications go through the town's SmartGov online portal, the Chief Building Official holds drop-in hours at Town Hall on Tuesdays from 12:30 to 1:30, and Bennett-Watkins Fire reviews fire requirements. The town is blunt about one thing: unpermitted work doubles the fees. We prepare the drawings, file through the portal, and manage inspections through final. If your parcel sits outside town limits, the county counter applies instead — [Adams County](/locations/adams-county) north of the line, Arapahoe County south — and we confirm which from the parcel record before we draw anything.

## Wind and hail country

There is nothing between Bennett and the Kansas line to slow the wind down, and the hail that crosses the eastern plains in summer is the kind that totals roofs. That shapes the engineering more than snow does here. Footings go below the 30–36 inch frost line and posts and beams are sized for Front Range snow, but any [covered patio or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) also gets uplift connections at every post, because a roof on an open lot wants to leave in a spring gust. Hail is why we lead with [capped composite](/services/composite-decks): it does not check, splinter, or gray the way softwood does after a few seasons of stones and high-altitude sun. We install Trex, Deckorators, and TimberTech as a certified dealer.

## Two kinds of houses, two kinds of decks

In Muegge Farms and the newer subdivisions, the deck is usually a replacement for the builder-grade wood platform that shipped with the house — small, sun-beaten, and ready to be something better, often with a shade structure that makes an open lot livable in July. In Old Town and on the acreages, the work is more custom: a deck sized to the yard, a railing that keeps the horizon in view, sometimes an outdoor kitchen because the nearest restaurant is a drive. Either way the free on-site consult is where the design starts, and we draw from the actual lot rather than a catalog.

## Repair or replace

We inspect the frame before quoting anything. Sound joists, posts, and a bolted ledger earn a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) in composite at $20–$35 per square foot; rot at grade, a nailed ledger, or footings above frost get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) recommendation, in writing. The inspection is free, and we photograph what we find.
