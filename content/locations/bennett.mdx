## The first town east on I-70

Bennett sits at about 5,480 feet on the prairie, a farm and railroad town of roughly 3,000 people that has become a commuter town. Old Town Bennett near the tracks is 1900s–70s houses on a small grid; Muegge Farms, Bennett Crossing, and Antelope Hills are 2010s–2020s production subdivisions on flat, wind-swept ground, and the Kiowa-Bennett corridor runs south into acreage. Town limits straddle the Adams/Arapahoe county line, which matters for a lot of things but not, inside town, for permits.

## The town issues its own permits

Unlike unincorporated [Watkins](/locations/watkins) and [Strasburg](/locations/strasburg) next door, which file with the counties, Bennett is an incorporated town with its own Building Department under Community Development. Applications go through the town's SmartGov online portal, and Bennett-Watkins Fire reviews fire requirements. Unpermitted work doubles the fees. We prepare the drawings, file through the portal, and manage inspections through final. If your parcel sits outside town limits, the county counter applies instead — [Adams County](/locations/adams-county) north of the line, Arapahoe County south — which we confirm from the parcel record first.

## Wind and hail country

There is nothing between Bennett and Kansas to slow the wind, and eastern-plains hail in summer is the kind that totals roofs. That shapes the engineering more than snow does here. Footings go below the 30–36 inch frost line and posts and beams are sized for Front Range snow, but any [covered patio or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) also gets uplift connections at every post, because an open-lot roof wants to leave in a spring gust. Hail is why we lead with [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) — Trex, Deckorators, TimberTech — it does not check, splinter, or gray the way softwood does after a few seasons of stones and high-altitude sun.

## Two kinds of houses, two kinds of decks

In Muegge Farms and the newer subdivisions, the deck is usually a replacement for the builder-grade wood platform that shipped with the house — small, sun-beaten, and ready to be something better, often with a shade structure that makes an open lot livable in July. In Old Town and on the acreages, the work is more custom: a deck sized to the yard, a railing that keeps the horizon in view, sometimes an outdoor kitchen because the nearest restaurant is a drive. Either way, design starts with a free on-site consult, from the actual lot, not a catalog.

## Repair or replace

We inspect the frame before quoting anything. Sound joists, posts, and a bolted ledger earn a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) in composite at $20–$35 per square foot; rot at grade, a nailed ledger, or footings above frost get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) recommendation, in writing. The inspection is free, and we photograph what we find.

Bennett is about forty minutes from our Centennial shop, straight out I-70 past [Aurora](/locations/aurora). The drive shapes scheduling, not the price or the warranty.
