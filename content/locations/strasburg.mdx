## A railroad town on the section line

Strasburg is an unincorporated I-70 railroad town of about 3,300 people at roughly 5,380 feet, forty-five minutes east of the Tech Center. Old Strasburg along Main Street is 1900s–70s houses on the original grid; Wolf Creek Run, Strasburg Estates, and the Comanche Crossing area are 1990s–2020s ranches and two-stories on one-to-five-acre lots. The ground is flat and fully exposed to wind and hail, and the Adams/Arapahoe county line runs through the community along a section line — which is the first thing we sort out on any project here.

## Which county, which counter

Strasburg has no town hall to file with. Parcels north of the line file with the Adams County Building Safety Division at the One Stop Customer Center in Brighton, which enforces the 2024 International codes as of July 1, 2026 and requires a permit for any deck over 30 inches above grade or with an attached roof. Parcels south of the line file with the Arapahoe County Building Division at Lima Plaza in Centennial, a few minutes from our shop. Both counties use Accela portals for applications. We pull the parcel record, confirm the county, and file at the right counter — then prepare the drawings and carry the permit through inspections. The [Adams County](/locations/adams-county) and [Arapahoe County](/locations/arapahoe-county) pages have the background.

## Built for the open sky

Wind is the load that governs a roof out here. Any [covered patio or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) gets uplift connections at every post and a frame braced to stay put, because there is nothing between Strasburg and the horizon to slow a spring gust. Hail governs the material: [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) takes the stones and the high-altitude UV without checking, splintering, or graying, and it needs a rinse instead of an annual stain. Snow still sizes the frame, and footings still go below the 30–36 inch frost line. We install Trex, Deckorators, and TimberTech as a certified dealer, and if you want real wood we will say honestly what that costs in upkeep on an exposed lot.

## Grid lots and acreage

In Old Strasburg the lot sets the footprint, and the deck is often a modest platform off the kitchen — done right, with stairs and railing that will pass inspection and hold up. On the acreages the deck is sized to the view: wraparounds, covered sections, railing chosen so the horizon stays in it rather than behind it. We design from the actual grade and the actual sun, and the free on-site consult is where that starts — we measure the lot, look at the exposure, and talk through what the deck is for before we draw anything.

## Repair or replace

Plenty of houses here carry original wood decks. We inspect the frame before quoting. Sound joists, posts, and a bolted ledger earn a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) at $20–$35 per square foot; rot at grade, a nailed ledger, or shallow footings get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) recommendation, in writing. The inspection is free, and we photograph what we find.
