## A master plan with a mountain in it

Ken Caryl is really two places sharing a name. The Plains side is classic 1970s–90s master-planned suburbia. The Valley sits behind the Dakota hogback in genuine foothills terrain, with 1980s–90s move-up and custom homes, and custom builds up in North Ranch. Same association, very different ground — and a deck detailed for a flat Plains lot is not automatically right for a Valley lot backing to open foothills.

We build on both sides, and we let the terrain drive the details.

## Two approvals, one sequence

Ken Caryl is unincorporated Jefferson County, so Jeffco Building Safety issues the permit. But nothing exterior starts without the Ken-Caryl Ranch Master Association Architectural Committee, which meets the second and fourth Thursdays of each month and reviews everything from footprint to whether the deck color complements the house. Approvals run six months. Sub-associations — the Enclave, Mountain Gate, the Settlement, Manor Ridge, Sunset Ridge, North Ranch — add their own layer where they apply.

None of this is a problem if it is sequenced correctly from the start. It is a schedule-killer if it is discovered late. Sequencing it is part of [our process](/process), and our [permit guide](/deck-permits-south-denver-metro) covers the county side.

## The wildfire code just changed the math

Jefferson County's Wildfire Resiliency Code took effect July 1, 2026. In mapped WUI zones of unincorporated Jeffco — which is parcel-specific, and which we confirm with the county for your address — new and replacement deck surfaces must be noncombustible, with ignition-limiting framing details underneath.

For Valley and foothill lots, that makes [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) more than our usual recommendation: it is the code-aligned way to build. We were already closing framing bays and keeping deck undersides clear of stored fuel on foothills builds; the code turns good practice into requirement.

## Composite for the rest of the reasons too

Even on Plains lots the code does not touch, the argument holds. Front Range sun at 5,800 feet is brutal on wood finishes, freeze-thaw cycles work every fastener, and hail finds every soft surface. Composite shrugs at all three, and modern [railing systems](/services/railings) match it for longevity. For shade and shoulder-season use, a [pergola or covered section](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) engineered for real Colorado snow load finishes the job — the [covered deck cost guide](/blog/covered-deck-cost-denver) has honest numbers.

## Repair when it is right, replacement when it is true

Much of Ken Caryl's housing went up thirty to fifty years ago, and plenty of original decks are still standing on original framing. We inspect before we recommend: sound structure gets a [repair](/services/deck-repair) or resurface, aged-out structure gets a straight answer and a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) plan. Our [deck safety inspection guide](/deck-safety-inspection-guide) shows you what we look for.
