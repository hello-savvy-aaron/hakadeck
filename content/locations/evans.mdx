## Greeley's south side, on its own terms

Evans is a home-rule city of about 22,000 on Greeley's southern edge, where the South Platte bends past at roughly 4,700 feet. The housing is mostly 1970s–2000s single-family ranches and two-stories on modest, flat lots — Ashcroft Heights, Grapevine Hollow, the streets around Riverside Park, and the older 37th Street corridor of Old Town Evans — with newer subdivisions like Tuscany filling in west of US 85. It is a working town with a lot of first-generation wood decks reaching the end of their run.

That is most of our work here: replacing a deck that was built in the 1980s or 1990s to a lighter standard, or building the first real one on a house that only ever had a concrete stoop.

## Permits: the Evans Building Division, not Greeley's

Evans runs its own Building Division at 1100 37th Street, separate from the [Greeley](/locations/greeley) counter next door and from the county. Applications go through the CitizenServe online portal, and the division works to the 2018 I-Codes with the 2023 NEC. We prepare the plans, file through CitizenServe, and manage every inspection through final. Parcels outside city limits file with [Weld County](/locations/weld-county), and we confirm the jurisdiction from your address before drawing anything.

## River-valley ground, plains weather

Evans does not publish its own design criteria; the regional Weld County values govern. Ground snow is 30 psf, wind is 115 mph three-second gust, and frost depth is 30 inches. Snow is rarely the limiting number on the plains. Wind is: a [pergola or patio cover](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) here is engineered for uplift, with post bases and roof connectors sized for it. Footings go below frost, drilled into the river-valley clay, because a shallow pad in this soil moves every spring.

The surface question is decided by hail and sun. Weld County is hail country, and a south-facing deck at this elevation takes UV that fails a wood finish in a couple of seasons. [Capped composite](/services/composite-decks) takes both and asks for a hose instead of a stain. If you are still weighing wood, we will lay out both sides honestly at the site visit.

## What we build in Evans

The common project is a 300–400 square foot composite deck on a flat lot, often with a covered section on the west or south side so it is usable in a July evening. On the older ranches we frequently pair a re-deck with new [railing](/services/railings), because the original wood two-by verticals are the first thing that fails and the first thing an inspector flags. We are a Trex, TimberTech, and Deckorators dealer, and everything carries our two-year workmanship warranty.

## Repair versus replace

Evans has a deep stock of 1970s–1990s decks, and the right answer is different house to house. We inspect the ledger, posts, footings, and hardware before quoting. Sound framing gets a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) at $20–$35 per square foot — new surface, new railing, same bones. A failed ledger or footings above frost get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) plan built to current code. We quote the repair against the cost of replacement so you can compare the two directly.
