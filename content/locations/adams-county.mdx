## The metro's north side

Adams County runs from the metro's inner ring out onto the plains northeast of Denver — Westminster and Northglenn against the Jefferson County line, Thornton up the I-25 corridor, Commerce City along the river and refineries, Brighton out where the county seat meets farmland that is quickly becoming subdivisions. The housing tells the same story in two chapters: a large cohort of 1960s and 70s ranches and split-levels in the first-ring suburbs, and newer production neighborhoods filling in toward Brighton.

## Where we build in Adams County

We maintain dedicated pages for the county's major cities, each with its own permitting and housing detail: [Thornton](/locations/thornton), the county's largest city and the heart of its I-25 growth corridor; [Northglenn](/locations/northglenn), a compact 1960s planned suburb where original decks are a fact of life; [Brighton](/locations/brighton), the county seat with the newest housing stock; [Commerce City](/locations/commerce-city), from the historic core to the fast-growing Reunion neighborhoods; and [Westminster](/locations/westminster), which straddles the Adams-Jefferson line with housing from every decade since the 1950s.

## A county of aging decks

The first-ring suburbs went up together, which means their decks are aging out together. A wood deck built with the house in 1972 — or even a replacement from the 1990s — is past the point where staining fixes anything. We inspect the structure before quoting: sound framing can carry a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) in new composite for meaningfully less than starting over, while failing ledgers or heaved footings make a [full replacement](/services/deck-replacement) the honest call. Our [deck safety inspection guide](/deck-safety-inspection-guide) covers what we look for. On the newer Brighton-side homes the work runs the other direction — builder-basic backyards getting their first real [composite deck](/services/composite-decks).

## Permits, city by city

Adams County permitting is a map question. Thornton, Northglenn, Brighton, Commerce City, and Westminster each run their own building departments — Northglenn's building services come through its contracted provider, SAFEbuilt — and unincorporated Adams County addresses file with the county's building department instead. Typical metro review runs one to three weeks with permit fees in the $150-500 range, and we prepare the drawings, file at the right counter, and manage inspections on every build. Our [permit guide](/deck-permits-south-denver-metro) covers the general process.

## What we build here

Most Adams County projects are [composite decks](/services/composite-decks) with [railing systems](/services/railings) chosen to fit the house, and a growing share add a [pergola or patio cover](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) for shade on treeless newer lots. Capped composite is our default for the same reasons it is everywhere on the Front Range: hail, altitude UV, and freeze-thaw are hard on wood, and composite answers all three with soap and water.
