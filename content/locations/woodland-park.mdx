## The City Above the Clouds

Woodland Park sits at 8,465 feet on the far side of Ute Pass, the biggest town in [Teller County](/locations/teller-county) and the highest place we build. The housing is mostly 1970s–2000s mountain homes on pine-covered lots — Paradise Estates, Sunnywood, Morning Sun, Trail Ridge, and the Rampart Range Road side of town — plus newer subdivisions, and nearly all of it has a Pikes Peak view. Two things shape every deck here: a snow season that runs roughly half the year, and elk that treat yards as a thoroughfare.

## Permits: the city first, then PPRBD

Step one is the City's Planning and Building Department at 220 W South Avenue, which issues a Multi-Use Zoning Development Permit — the setback review. Step two is the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department, which takes the construction drawings after city sign-off, issues the building permit, and runs inspections — the same counter as Colorado Springs and [Green Mountain Falls](/locations/green-mountain-falls). Within Teller County, PPRBD serves only the city, so addresses outside city limits file with the Teller County Building Division instead. We confirm which applies to your parcel, prepare one packet, and carry it through final.

## Fifty-seven psf, and a long winter

Above the 7,000-foot line, PPRBD's design criteria step up to 57 psf ground snow and 40 psf flat-roof snow; Teller County's own code uses the same figures with a 30-inch minimum frost depth. That number sizes every beam, post, and footing under a [covered deck or patio cover](/services/pergolas-patio-covers), and at 8,465 feet it is not conservative — it is the winter. Snow that lands in November is still there in April. An older uncovered deck framed to a lighter assumption is worth checking before the next big storm. A covered section turns a Woodland Park deck from a four-month room into a year-round one.

## Composite, at 8,465 feet

Altitude UV, hail, months of ice, and daily freeze-thaw: this is the weather that wrecks wood finishes in a couple of summers. [Capped composite](/services/composite-decks) handles all of it with soap and water. And because the view is the point on most of these lots, a slim aluminum or cable railing keeps Pikes Peak in the frame.

## Repair or replace

Many Woodland Park decks date to the 1970s–1990s, built before the current snow tier and hardware standards. We inspect the frame before quoting. Sound structure earns a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) in composite at $20–$35 per square foot; an undersized frame or shallow footings get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) recommendation in writing. We photograph what we find and explain the call.

## Ninety-five minutes, in blocks

From our shop in [Centennial](/locations/centennial), Woodland Park is about ninety-five minutes — I-25 south, US-24 up through Ute Pass past [Cascade](/locations/cascade). Mountain work is scheduled in blocks, with site visits and build days run back to back. Price and the warranty do not change; lead time does, and the short season rewards planning early — fall contracts get the first spring build slots.
