## Deck installation in Aurora

If you're comparing quotes for deck installation in Aurora, it helps to know what a correct install actually involves — because the decking you'll walk on is the last and easiest part.

It starts underground. Colorado's frost line sits 30 to 36 inches down, and every post needs an engineered concrete footing that reaches below it. A footing that stops short heaves with the freeze-thaw cycle and takes the deck's level — and eventually its railing lines and stair landings — along with it. Footing depth and diameter go on the drawings the City of Aurora reviews before we dig, so they're engineering decisions, not guesses made in the hole.

Framing comes next: the ledger flashed and bolted to the house, joists sized and spaced to the tighter span requirements composite demands, and solid blocking wherever railing posts and stairs will load the structure. Then the [composite installation](/services/composite-decks) itself — hidden fasteners, correct gapping so boards can move with Aurora's temperature swings, and picture-frame borders where the design calls for them. Most Aurora decks run one to three weeks on site once the permit clears; design and city review happen before the crew arrives, so the disruptive part of the project is measured in days, not months.

Not every Aurora deck needs installing from scratch, either. The city's older housing stock on the metro's east side carries plenty of decks that are past their surface but not their structure, and there an honest [deck repair](/services/deck-repair) or re-deck quote beats a replacement pitch. One symptom worth reading about before you call anyone: boards curling up at the edges. Our guide to [why deck boards cup](/blog/deck-boards-cupping) explains which cases a fastener fix solves — and which mean the surface is done, even when the framing underneath is worth keeping.

## Hail alley's material problem

Southeast Aurora sits squarely in one of the most hail-prone corridors in the country — the same storms that keep the region's roofers busy are brutal on stained and painted wood decks. It's the single strongest argument for [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) in this part of the metro: the polymer cap takes hail strikes without the pitting and finish damage that send wood decks into an annual sand-and-restain cycle. Pair it with a [pergola or solid-roof cover](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) and the deck stays usable when the weather turns — which, on the high plains east of the city, it does with enthusiasm.

## Two generations of decks, one fix

Aurora's southeast filled in across two waves — the 1990s–2000s neighborhoods are now replacing their original builder-grade decks, while newer communities are upgrading platforms that were always too small for how the houses actually live. Both projects end the same way: an engineered, permitted deck sized for real use. Our [two-tier composite build](/portfolio/double-decker) just across the Centennial line is the template for a lot of what we do here — outdoor dining up top, shaded lounge below, and [railing](/services/railings) chosen to keep the plains view rather than block it.

When a deck is tired but its bones are good, the honest answer is often cheaper than a rebuild. Wood deck repair in Aurora is a steady part of our week — we inspect the ledger, footings, and framing first, then quote a [repair or re-deck](/services/deck-repair) when that's genuinely the right call and a full [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) when it isn't. The diagnosis is free either way, and it comes with photos of what we found under your deck, not just a number.
