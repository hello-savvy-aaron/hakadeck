## A historic town with new edges

Platteville sits on US 85 at about 4,820 feet, a town of roughly 3,000 on flat river-valley ground with the Fort Vasquez trading-post museum on its south edge. The housing runs in three layers: early-1900s homes in Old Town, 1970s–2000s ranches between them and the highway, and 2010s–2020s subdivisions — Pelican Lake Ranch, Gateway — on the edges, where lots are level and there is not a mature tree to shade a south-facing yard.

Each layer needs something different. Downtown, it is usually a small deck or porch replaced within a tight setback. In the newer subdivisions, it is upgrading the code-minimum builder deck or building the first real one.

## Permits through Town Hall, reviewed by SAFEbuilt

The Town of Platteville handles permits through Town Hall at 400 Grand Avenue, and completed applications are emailed to the town's permit contact. SAFEbuilt plans examiners review the submittal and conduct the inspections on the town's behalf. Decks and covered patios both require a permit here, and the town posts an Uncovered Decks & Porches guide that tells you what the reviewer expects. We draw the plans, submit them, and run inspections through final. Parcels outside town limits file with [Weld County](/locations/weld-county), and we confirm the jurisdiction from your address first.

## River-valley ground, plains weather

Platteville publishes no design criteria of its own, so the regional Weld County values govern: 30 psf ground snow, 115 mph three-second-gust wind, and a 30-inch frost depth. Wind is the structural number on these lots. A [covered patio or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) is engineered for uplift, with roof connectors and post bases sized to hold in a spring gale. Footings are drilled below frost into river-valley clay that heaves a shallow pad every year.

Hail and sun decide the surface. Weld County is hail country, and a south-facing deck at this elevation takes UV that fails a wood finish in two seasons. [Capped composite](/services/composite-decks) takes both without a refinishing schedule. If you are still weighing wood, we will lay out both sides honestly at the site visit.

## What we build in Platteville

In Pelican Lake Ranch and Gateway, the typical project pairs a 300–400 square foot composite deck with a covered section so it is usable in July heat — designed as one structure rather than a cover bolted on later. Slim aluminum [railing](/services/railings) keeps the open view these lots have. In Old Town, the work is fitting a useful deck inside a small setback and matching the scale of an older house. We price the cover as its own line so you can see exactly what the shade costs. We are a Trex, TimberTech, and Deckorators dealer, and every build carries our two-year workmanship warranty.

## Repair or replace

Between the 1970s ranches and the 2010s subdivisions, Platteville has plenty of decks whose surfaces have failed on frames that have not. We inspect the ledger, posts, footings, and hardware first. Sound structure gets a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) at $20–$35 per square foot; a failed ledger or footings above frost get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) plan. The findings go to you in writing, with photos.

Platteville is about fifty-five minutes from our Centennial shop, straight up US 85 past [Fort Lupton](/locations/fort-lupton) and short of [Gilcrest](/locations/gilcrest). We block these US 85 towns together so a crew is on site for consecutive days. The drive shapes scheduling, not the price or the guarantee.
