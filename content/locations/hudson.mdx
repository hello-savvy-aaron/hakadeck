## A plains town east of Brighton

Hudson sits on I-76 east of Brighton at about 5,020 feet, a town of roughly 1,700 on open plains. The core is a compact early-1900s grid in Old Town; around it are 1970s–2000s ranches and a growing number of 2010s–2020s subdivisions — Hudson Hills, Box Elder Creek Ranch — plus rural acreages where the deck is often the best room on the property. Nothing breaks the wind out here, and nothing shades a south-facing yard.

That mix means two kinds of work. On the grid, it is replacing a small older deck within a tight lot. On the acreages and in the newer subdivisions, it is a bigger deck with a covered section, often the first real outdoor room the house has had.

## Permits through the Town Clerk, reviewed by ProCode

The Town of Hudson issues building permits through the Town Clerk at 50 South Beech Street, after ProCode — the town's contracted Chief Building Official — reviews the plans. ProCode also performs the inspections, scheduled through the Community Core portal. The town currently enforces the 2018 ICC codes with 2024 fire-code amendments and has been moving to the 2024 I-Codes, so we draw to the newer edition to stay ahead of the change. Anything touching the right-of-way needs a separate ROW permit. We prepare the plans, file, and manage inspections through final. Acreage outside town limits files with [Weld County](/locations/weld-county), and we confirm the jurisdiction from your parcel first.

## Wind first, then frost

Hudson publishes no design criteria of its own, so the regional Weld County values apply: 30 psf ground snow, 115 mph three-second-gust wind, and a 30-inch frost depth. On these plains, wind is the structural number. A [pergola or patio cover](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) is engineered for uplift, with roof connectors and post bases sized to hold in a spring gale rather than picked from a catalog. Footings are drilled below frost into clay that heaves a shallow pad every year.

Hail and sun decide the surface. Weld County is hail country, and a south-facing deck at this elevation takes UV that fails a wood finish in two seasons. [Capped composite](/services/composite-decks) takes both and asks for a hose instead of a stain. If you are still weighing wood, we will lay out both sides honestly at the site visit.

## What we build in Hudson

On the acreages and in Hudson Hills and Box Elder Creek Ranch, the typical project is a 300–400 square foot composite deck or larger with a solid cover so it is usable in July heat and a March wind, designed as one structure. Slim aluminum [railing](/services/railings) keeps the long view these lots have. On the grid, the work is fitting a useful deck inside a small setback and matching the scale of an older house. We price the cover as its own line so you can see exactly what the shade costs. We are a Trex, TimberTech, and Deckorators dealer, and every build carries our two-year workmanship warranty.

## Repair or replace

Hudson has plenty of decks built decades ago to a lighter standard. We inspect the ledger, posts, footings, and hardware before quoting. Sound framing gets a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) at $20–$35 per square foot; a failed ledger or footings above frost get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) plan built to current code. The findings go to you in writing, with photos.
