## The far edge of Arapahoe County

Deer Trail is a statutory town of about 600 people at roughly 5,180 feet, sixty-five minutes east of the Tech Center on I-70. The townsite along 2nd Avenue is early-1900s cottages and 1950s–70s ranches on a small grid; the I-70 frontage and the East Bijou Creek acreages around it are ranch and ag country. It is the farthest east we build in Arapahoe County, and the deck work here is exactly what the landscape suggests: open sky, long views, and a frame that has to hold still in the wind. It is also the kind of place where a deck gets used, because the evenings are the best part of the day.

## Town Clerk first, county as fallback

Inside town limits, Deer Trail takes building permit applications through an online form on the town website, and questions go to the Town Clerk at Town Hall, 555 2nd Ave, open Monday through Friday from 8 to 4. Parcels outside town limits are unincorporated Arapahoe County and file with the county Building Division at Lima Plaza in Centennial instead. We confirm which applies from your parcel, prepare the drawings, submit, and manage inspections through final. The [Arapahoe County](/locations/arapahoe-county) page covers how the county counter works.

## Wind, hail, sun

The loads that matter in Deer Trail are the ones the open sky delivers. Wind governs anything with a roof: a [covered patio or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) gets uplift connections at every post and a frame braced to stay put in a spring gust. Hail governs the material: [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) takes the stones and the high-altitude UV without checking, splintering, or graying, and needs a rinse instead of an annual stain. Snow still sizes the frame, and footings still go below the 30–36 inch frost line. We install Trex, Deckorators, and TimberTech as a certified dealer, and if you want real wood we will say honestly what it costs to keep up out here.

## Small-town grid, big acreage

On the Deer Trail grid the lot sets the footprint, and the deck is usually a well-built platform off the back of a cottage or ranch — stairs and railing that will pass inspection and last, sized to a small yard. On the acreages the deck is sized to the view: a wraparound, a covered section for the hours when the sun is straight overhead, a railing that keeps the horizon in front of you. We design from the actual grade and the actual sun, and the free on-site consult is where that starts.

## Repair or replace

Plenty of homes here carry original wood decks. We inspect the frame before quoting. Sound joists, posts, and a bolted ledger earn a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) at $20–$35 per square foot; rot at grade, a nailed ledger, or shallow footings get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) recommendation, in writing. The inspection is free, and we photograph what we find.

## An hour east, planned honestly

Deer Trail is about sixty-five minutes from our Centennial shop — past [Byers](/locations/byers) and [Strasburg](/locations/strasburg), a bit short of [Limon](/locations/limon). At that distance we group site visits and plan build weeks so the crew is not driving twice for one day's work, and we say so up front. The price and the warranty do not change. Run the [cost calculator](/deck-cost-calculator) for a planning range, then [tell us what you are planning](/contact) and we will come walk the ground.
