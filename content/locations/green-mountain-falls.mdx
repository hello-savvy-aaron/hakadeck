## A valley town on the county line

Green Mountain Falls is small — about 875 people — and it sits in a shaded valley at 7,800 feet with Pike National Forest on every side. The 1890 gazebo on Gazebo Lake is the center of town; the housing climbs away from it up Lake Street, Ute Pass Avenue, the Crystal Falls area, and the Hondo Avenue slopes. It is a mix of rustic log cabins, Victorian homes, and modern builds on steep, wooded lots, and a lot of it is summer-only. The town straddles the El Paso–Teller county line, which sounds like a permit headache and, for building permits at least, is not.

## Permits: the town first, then PPRBD

Most construction in Green Mountain Falls needs town approval before anything else — from Town staff, the Planning Commission, or the Board of Trustees, through a site plan and an architectural or zoning plan review application. Once the town signs off, the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department issues the building permit and runs inspections, the same counter that serves Colorado Springs and the rest of [El Paso County](/locations/el-paso-county). PPRBD handles building permits for the whole town regardless of which side of the [Teller County](/locations/teller-county) line a parcel sits on. We manage both steps, prepare one clean packet, and carry inspections through final.

## Snow in a shaded valley

At 7,800 feet the town is well above the 7,000-foot line where PPRBD's design criteria step up to 57 psf ground snow and 40 psf flat-roof snow. That number sizes every beam, post, and footing under a [covered deck or patio cover](/services/pergolas-patio-covers), and the valley makes it real: with the sun blocked by ridges for much of the winter, snow that lands in December stays until spring rather than melting off between storms. Footings go below the 30–36 inch frost line. If you own an older cabin deck and have watched it sag under drifts, that load is the reason.

## Composite, for the freeze and the shade

Months of ice and thaw on a shaded deck are exactly what breaks down wood: finishes fail, boards check, fasteners work loose. [Capped composite](/services/composite-decks) handles moisture and freeze-thaw without a staining schedule, and it stands up to the altitude UV where the sun does reach. Where the deck looks down the valley or up at the forest, a slim aluminum or cable railing keeps the view open.

## Cabin decks: repair or replace

Many decks here were built for summer cabins, with lighter snow assumptions and hardware the code no longer accepts. We inspect the structure before quoting. A sound ledger, posts, and footings earn a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) at $20–$35 per square foot; an undersized frame or footings that never reached frost depth get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) recommendation in writing. Rebuilding once beats repairing twice at this elevation, and we say so when that is the honest answer.

## Eighty-five minutes, scheduled in blocks

From our shop in [Centennial](/locations/centennial), Green Mountain Falls is about eighty-five minutes — I-25 south, then US-24 up Ute Pass past [Cascade](/locations/cascade). We are plain about the distance: mountain work gets scheduled in blocks, with batched site visits and build days run back to back rather than one-off trips.
