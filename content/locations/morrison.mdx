## Two Morrisons, one zip code

The Town of Morrison is a few blocks of 1870s buildings at the mouth of Bear Creek Canyon, around 5,765 feet. Nearly everything else that carries a Morrison address — the custom homes around Red Rocks Country Club in Willow Springs, Willowbrook, and Lyons Ridge; the starter homes of Friendly Hills and Harriman Park; the cabins up in Hilldale Pines — is unincorporated Jefferson County, climbing into foothills that reach about 7,500 feet.

That split is not trivia. It decides who permits your deck, what code applies, and increasingly what your deck surface is allowed to be made of.

## Permits: town counter or county counter

Inside town limits, the Town of Morrison Building Department is your counter, and decks are explicitly permit-required. Everywhere else, it is Jeffco Building Safety. We confirm jurisdiction from your parcel before drawing a line — it is a five-minute check that prevents a five-week mistake — then prepare the packet, file it, and manage inspections through final. Our [permit guide](/deck-permits-south-denver-metro) covers the wider process.

## The wildfire code, plainly

Jefferson County's Wildfire Resiliency Code took effect July 1, 2026. In mapped WUI zones of unincorporated Jeffco, new and replacement deck surfaces must be noncombustible, with ignition-limiting framing underneath. Whether it applies to you is parcel-specific — but Morrison's foothill neighborhoods are exactly the terrain the mapping targets, so we check with the county at the start of every project here. On mapped lots, [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) is now the code-aligned way to build, not just our preference.

## Hogback terrain earns its engineering

Red-rock and hogback ground makes for spectacular decks and honest structural work: sloped lots want taller posts, engineered footings, and stair runs that work with the grade. West-facing foothill exposure also means real sun and real weather — which is where a [covered section or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) engineered for wind and snow load earns its keep. The [covered deck cost guide](/blog/covered-deck-cost-denver) has honest numbers on what that adds.

## Composite first, for Morrison reasons

Altitude UV fades and checks wood finishes fast, freeze-thaw works fasteners loose every winter, and hail is a summer certainty. Composite handles all three with near-zero upkeep, and modern [railing](/services/railings) systems match its lifespan. If you are weighing the alternatives honestly, our [composite vs. hardwood comparison](/composite-vs-hardwood-decking-colorado) lays out both sides.

## Repair when true, replacement when honest

Morrison's decks are as varied as its housing — some are ten years old, some are original to 1970s homes. We inspect the frame first: sound structure gets a [repair](/services/deck-repair), aged-out structure gets a plain answer and a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) plan. No drama either way.
