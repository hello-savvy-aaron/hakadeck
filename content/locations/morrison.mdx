## Two Morrisons, one zip code

The Town of Morrison is a few blocks of 1870s buildings at the mouth of Bear Creek Canyon. Nearly everything else that carries a Morrison address — the custom homes around Red Rocks Country Club in Willow Springs, Willowbrook, and Lyons Ridge; the starter homes of Friendly Hills and Harriman Park; the cabins up in Hilldale Pines — is unincorporated Jefferson County, climbing into foothills that reach about 7,500 feet.

That split matters: it decides who permits your deck, what code applies, and increasingly what your deck surface is allowed to be made of.

## Permits: town counter or county counter

Inside town limits, the Town of Morrison Building Department is your counter, and decks are explicitly permit-required. Everywhere else, it is Jeffco Building Safety. We confirm jurisdiction from your parcel before drawing a line, then prepare the packet, file it, and manage inspections. Our [permit guide](/deck-permits-south-denver-metro) covers the wider process.

On the county side, every new deck, expansion, replacement, and repair needs a building permit under Jeffco's 2018 IRC. Footings go to the county's 36-inch frost depth, deck live load starts at 40 psf and rises with roof snow load, and joist cantilevers over two feet need a Colorado registered design professional's seal. The town's posted deck handout is simpler — two plot plans, 36-inch guards that stop a 4-inch sphere, piers 36 inches deep — but the permit is still required.

## The wildfire code, plainly

Jefferson County's Wildfire Resiliency Code took effect July 1, 2026. In mapped WUI zones of unincorporated Jeffco it covers new decks and deck replacements: the deck surface has to be noncombustible, an approved composite with a flame-spread index of 75 or less, or another ignition-resistant material, and the framing underneath has its own list — heavier-dimension lumber, fire-retardant-treated wood, or noncombustible framing. Whether it applies to you is parcel-specific — but Morrison's foothill neighborhoods are the terrain the mapping targets. The county's interactive WUI map shows whether an address falls in a Class 1 or Class 2 area, and we check it before quoting materials. On mapped lots, [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) is now the code-aligned way to build, not just our preference.

## Hogback terrain earns its engineering

Red-rock and hogback ground makes for spectacular decks and honest structural work: sloped lots want taller posts, engineered footings, and stair runs that work with the grade. West-facing foothill exposure also means real sun and real weather — which is where a [covered section or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) engineered for wind and snow load earns its keep. The [covered deck cost guide](/blog/covered-deck-cost-denver) has numbers on what that adds.

## Snow load climbs with the hill

Jefferson County sets design snow load by elevation. Below 6,500 feet the ground snow load is 43 psf; at 7,000 feet it is 54; at 7,500 feet, 67. Past 70 psf a licensed design professional has to stamp the plans. A deck in town and a deck a thousand feet higher are not the same structure. Our [snow load guide](/blog/how-much-snow-can-my-deck-hold) explains what those numbers mean for beams and joists.

## Composite first, for Morrison reasons

Altitude UV fades wood finishes fast, freeze-thaw works fasteners loose, and hail is a summer certainty. Composite handles all three with near-zero upkeep, and [railing](/services/railings) systems match its lifespan. If you're weighing alternatives, our [composite vs. hardwood comparison](/composite-vs-hardwood-decking-colorado) lays out both sides.

## Repair when true, replacement when honest

Morrison's decks are as varied as its housing — some are ten years old, some are original to 1970s homes. We inspect the frame first: sound structure gets a [repair](/services/deck-repair), aged-out structure gets a plain answer and a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) plan.

Jeffco treats a repair like a build — it needs a permit too — so we file for it the same way we do up the hill in [Evergreen](/locations/evergreen). The [deck repair cost guide](/blog/deck-repair-cost-denver) shows what ledger, post, and stair work runs.
