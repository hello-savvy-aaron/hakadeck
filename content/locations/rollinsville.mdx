## A railroad hamlet on the Peak to Peak

Rollinsville sits at 8,474 feet on the Peak to Peak Highway, between [Nederland](/locations/nederland) to the north and [Black Hawk](/locations/black-hawk) to the south, just below the east portal of the Moffat Tunnel. The townsite along Highway 119 is a cluster of older frame homes and cabins; around it, mountain homes sit on multi-acre forested lots along South Boulder Creek and up the Tolland and East Portal road. About 194 people live here. The county is drafting a Rollinsville Area Specific Plan, with public meetings in October 2026, but for a deck the facts are already set: altitude, forest, snow, and a county that engineers everything.

## Permits: Gilpin County, through OpenGov

Rollinsville is unincorporated, so [Gilpin County](/locations/gilpin-county) Community Development's Building Division issues the permit through the county's OpenGov portal. Attached decks require a permit, and any change to posts, beams, joists, guards, or stairs on an existing deck is permitted work too. Inspections run Mondays and Wednesdays. The county has adopted the 2024 IRC and IFC and has issued its own electrical permits since March 2023, so a lighting circuit on the deck goes through the same counter. Timing matters more here than in the metro: applications filed after September 1 may not be approved before frost conditions, so we push Rollinsville contracts toward spring and summer submittals. We prepare the packet, file it, and manage inspections through final.

## No single snow-load number — engineered, every time

Gilpin County does not publish one snow-load figure. It publishes a Snow Loads of Gilpin County GIS layer, updated November 2024, and requires an ASCE Hazard Tool report at Risk Category II plus site-specific snow and wind design on every structural permit. Prescriptive deck tables generally do not qualify; the county's own language is that a licensed structural engineer is almost always required. We pull the value for your parcel from the county layer, have the structure engineered to it, and design any [covered deck or patio cover](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) as the roof it is.

## The wildfire code applies here

Gilpin County has adopted the 2024 International Wildland-Urban Interface Code with local amendments and the 2025 Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code, and the forested lots along South Boulder Creek are exactly what those codes are written for. Sprinklers are required for dwellings over 3,600 square feet and in the county's Extreme Building Exposure Areas. For a deck, the practical upshot is [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) or mineral-based boards instead of untreated softwood, framing bays closed or screened against embers, and an underside that is not a woodpile. We build that way on every Rollinsville parcel and confirm the exposure-area mapping with the county first.

## Multi-acre lots, real structure

Acreage sounds roomy until you find the buildable ground. Rollinsville lots run to slope and rock, and an elevated deck off the back of a cabin is often the best flat space on the property, with the budget's center of gravity in the posts and footings below. We design from the actual grade, and choose slim aluminum or cable railing that keeps the creek and the trees in view.

## Repair versus replace

Cabin-era decks here were built to assumptions a county reviewer would not accept today. We inspect the frame and answer plainly: sound structure earns a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) in ignition-resistant composite; aged-out structure gets a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) plan on engineered framing. Either way the answer comes in writing, and either way the county wants to see it.

## Eighty minutes, scheduled in blocks

From our Centennial shop, Rollinsville is about eighty minutes — up I-70 and US-6 through [Golden](/locations/golden) and Clear Creek Canyon, then north on the Peak to Peak. It is near the far edge of our range, so we schedule mountain builds in consecutive-day blocks rather than day trips. That keeps the drive out of your price; the guarantee is the same as it is in the metro.
