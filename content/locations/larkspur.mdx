## High ground between the metro and the Springs

Larkspur is a tiny town — a core of maybe a few hundred residents — surrounded by some of the best custom-home country on the Front Range. Perry Park's 750-plus families live among genuine red-rock formations near Lake Wauconda and the country club; Sage Port runs 240 one-acre covenant lots through the pines. Almost everything here is custom, treed, and sloped.

It's also 6,733 feet up on the Palmer Divide, and that elevation is the first thing we design for.

## Snow load is not a detail here

The Palmer Divide is an upslope-snow hotspot — the same storms that dust Denver stack feet here. Douglas County writes that into code by tiering snow-load criteria with elevation, from a 30 psf floor scaling to 70 psf at 9,500 feet and above, and Larkspur sits above the floor tier. Practically, that means beams, posts, and footings sized heavier than a metro deck, frost-depth footings at 30–36 inches, and [covered structures](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) engineered for snow bearing down and wind pulling up rather than picked from a catalog.

Sloped, treed lots add their own structural work: stepped footings, taller posts, and decks that earn their views by cantilevering over grade honestly.

## Ember-aware in Perry Park country

The fire context here is documented, not hypothetical. Perry Park maintains formal Community Wildfire Protection Plans citing its ponderosa and Gambel oak fuels, and the 2002 Hayman Fire burned to within miles. A deck is one of the most ember-exposed assemblies on a house, so on these lots we default to ignition-resistant surfaces, framing bays closed or screened so embers can't nest, and an underside that isn't a woodpile. None of it makes a deck fireproof — it makes it meaningfully less of a liability.

## Composite, for reasons this ridge supplies

Altitude UV, deep freeze-thaw cycling, heavy spring snows, and summer hail: this is the exact weather [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) was built for, and why we lead with it over wood that starts an annual refinishing clock at this elevation. Our [materials comparison](/composite-vs-hardwood-decking-colorado) is honest about both sides. Where red-rock views are the point — and in Perry Park they usually are — slim aluminum or cable [railing](/services/railings) keeps them.

## Existing decks get inspected first

Custom homes here span decades, and many carry original decks framed to lighter snow assumptions. We look at the structure before quoting: sound bones earn a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) in composite for meaningfully less than a teardown, while an undersized frame gets a straight [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) recommendation on current-code structure — in writing either way. Our [inspection guide](/deck-safety-inspection-guide) shows what we check.

## Two permit counters, sorted for you

Inside town limits, the Town of Larkspur's building department (run through SAFEbuilt) issues the permit. Perry Park, Sage Port, and most Larkspur addresses are unincorporated Douglas County — the county Building Division, same as our [Castle Rock](/locations/castle-rock) and [Castle Pines](/locations/castle-pines) work. We confirm which counter your parcel answers to, then prepare, file, and run inspections through sign-off. Background in our [permit guide](/deck-permits-south-denver-metro).
