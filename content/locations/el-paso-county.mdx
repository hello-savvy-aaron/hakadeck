faqs:
  - q: "Who issues deck permits in El Paso County?"
    a: "Nearly everyone here files through one counter: the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department issues residential permits regionally, so Colorado Springs, Monument, Palmer Lake, and unincorporated El Paso County all go through PPRBD under the 2023 Pikes Peak Regional Building Code. That is genuinely simpler than the Denver metro, where every suburb runs its own department. We prepare the packet, file it with PPRBD, and manage inspections through final."
  - q: "What does a deck cost in El Paso County?"
    a: "Capped composite runs about $40–$70 per square foot installed, which puts a typical 300–400 square foot deck around $15,000–$30,000 with railing and stairs. Sites at or above 7,000 feet carry heavier snow-load requirements, which can push covered structures toward the upper half of the range. Our calculator gives you a planning range in thirty seconds."
  - q: "Why does 7,000 feet matter for a deck here?"
    a: "PPRBD sets its snow-load criteria in two tiers that step up at 7,000 feet — 43/30 psf below the line, 57/40 psf at or above it — and Monument and the Palmer Divide neighborhoods straddle exactly that boundary. Two houses a mile apart can carry different structural requirements for the same covered deck. We check your elevation, then size beams, posts, and footings to the tier your site is actually in."
  - q: "Do you really serve the whole county?"
    a: "Honestly, we serve the I-25 corridor end of it — Monument, the Tri-Lakes area, and Colorado Springs — which is about an hour from our Centennial shop. The drive means we batch site visits and plan build weeks a little further ahead than we do near home, and price and warranty are the same as everywhere we build. If you are further east in the county, ask; the answer is usually yes with a longer lead."

## The far end of the Front Range run

El Paso County is Colorado's most populous county and the southern end of our range, down I-25 past the Palmer Divide. The housing skews newer than the Denver suburbs: whole subdivisions in northern Colorado Springs and the Tri-Lakes corridor have gone up in the last two decades, many shipped with builder-grade wood decks at elevations where snow and wind are structural facts, not footnotes.

## Where we build in El Paso County

[Monument](/locations/monument) is the heart of our El Paso County work — Tri-Lakes subdivisions sitting right on the Palmer Divide, where elevation drives the engineering. [Colorado Springs](/locations/colorado-springs) is the anchor at the south end, from established Rockrimmon and Broadmoor-area homes to the new neighborhoods spreading north toward the county line.

## Permits: one regional counter

The Pikes Peak Regional Building Department issues residential permits regionally — Colorado Springs, Monument, Palmer Lake, and unincorporated El Paso County all file through PPRBD under the 2023 Pikes Peak Regional Building Code. After working across a dozen separate metro-Denver building departments, one counter with one rulebook is a genuine simplification. Drawings, submittal, inspections, corrections: all part of the build, as our [process](/process) page lays out.

## The 7,000-foot line

The number that matters most in this county is elevation. PPRBD's snow-load criteria step up at 7,000 feet — 43/30 psf below the line, 57/40 psf at or above it — and Monument and the Palmer Divide neighborhoods straddle exactly that boundary. A [covered deck or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) on the high side of the line carries meaningfully more load, which means bigger beams, closer posts, and deeper pockets of concrete. We check the tier for your site before drawing.

## Wind, sun, and new subdivisions

The Divide funnels real wind across this county, and open new-build lots feel all of it — uplift on covered structures is engineered here. The same openness means sun: young Tri-Lakes subdivisions have little mature shade, so built shade earns its keep fast. The most common project here is upgrading a minimum-spec builder deck to an engineered [composite deck](/services/composite-decks) that handles hail, UV, and freeze-thaw with soap and water. Where an existing frame is sound, a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) is the honest quote; where it is not, we price a proper [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) and say why.

## An hour down the highway

From our shop in [Centennial](/locations/centennial), Monument is about fifty minutes and Colorado Springs an hour. We batch El Paso County site visits and plan build weeks accordingly — that is the whole effect of the distance. Price and [warranty](/warranty) do not change with the mile marker.
