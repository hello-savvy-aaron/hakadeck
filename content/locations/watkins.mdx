## A crossroads on the county line

Watkins is an unincorporated prairie crossroads at I-70 and Watkins Road, about 5,500 feet up and thirty-five minutes east of the Tech Center, with a few hundred residents in 1960s–90s homes spread across one-to-35-acre horse and ag lots along Box Elder Creek and out toward Front Range Airport. Aurora's metro-district subdivisions are creeping east toward it from the Aurora Highlands edge, so the area is changing, but most of the deck work here is still acreage work: a house with a long view and nothing between it and the weather.

## The parcel decides the permit counter

There is no Town of Watkins, and the Adams/Arapahoe county line runs through the community, so the county your parcel sits in decides where the permit goes. North of I-70 it is the Adams County Building Safety Division at the One Stop Customer Center in Brighton, which requires a permit for any deck over 30 inches above grade or with an attached roof and enforces the 2024 International codes as of July 1, 2026. South of I-70 it is the Arapahoe County Building Division at Lima Plaza in Centennial, a few minutes from our shop. Both counties take applications through Accela portals. We confirm the county from your parcel record before we draw a line, then prepare the packet, file at the right counter, and run inspections through final. Bennett-Watkins Fire serves the area. Background on the [Adams County](/locations/adams-county) and [Arapahoe County](/locations/arapahoe-county) pages.

## Wind first, then hail, then snow

On open prairie the governing loads are not the ones metro homeowners think about. Wind is first: a [covered patio or pergola](/services/pergolas-patio-covers) here gets uplift connections at every post and a frame braced to stay put in a spring gust, because there is nothing to break the wind for miles. Hail is second, and it is why we lead with [capped composite](/services/composite-decks) — softwood checks, splinters, and grays after a few seasons of stones and high-altitude UV, while composite takes the hit and needs a rinse. Snow is third; the frame is sized for Front Range loads and footings go below the 30–36 inch frost line. We install Trex, Deckorators, and TimberTech as a certified dealer.

## Acreage decks

An acreage deck is sized to the house and the view rather than to a setback, which means the design questions are about proportion, shade, and what you look through. Slim aluminum or cable railing keeps the horizon; a covered section makes the deck usable when the sun is straight overhead; a wraparound often earns its cost by giving a ranch house an outdoor room on two sides. We design from the actual grade and the actual sun.

## Repair or replace

Plenty of 1960s–90s houses here carry original wood decks. We inspect the frame before quoting. Sound joists, posts, and a bolted ledger earn a [re-deck](/services/deck-repair) at $20–$35 per square foot; rot, a nailed ledger, or shallow footings get a [replacement](/services/deck-replacement) recommendation, in writing. The inspection is free.
